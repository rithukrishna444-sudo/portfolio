01. About Me

I am an Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) undergraduate deeply fascinated by the convergence of hardware architectures, low-level operating system internals, and ultra-low-latency computing.

My passion lies in writing deterministic, cache-friendly C++ systems for High-Frequency Trading (HFT) simulations—maximizing throughput and minimizing nanosecond jitter. Concurrently, I build firmware for microcontrollers (STM32, ARM Cortex) and experiment with digital logic synthesis on FPGAs.

When I'm not profiling CPU cache misses or debugging hardware protocols like SPI, I2C, and CAN, you'll find me exploring computer architecture, OS kernels, and quantitative finance concepts.
